Engineered scalable, secure ETL/ELT pipelines to ingest and process up to 10 years of historical tax, medical, and financial data per client, reducing document processing times from weeks to days.

Implemented rigid data privacy protocols and masking rules to protect sensitive PII and protected medical health records, aligned with Canadian data residency and privacy expectations.
